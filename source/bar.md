# bar.md — what makes the reference work

Reference: "AI Chat Bot Product Design" by Figmatica Team (Behance gallery 192000613), from the five screenshots and the GIF the client supplied. Every line is checkable by looking at a render.

1. **Ground.** The page ground is a warm near-black (about #111–#161616), flat, with faint thin contour or map lines at under 10% contrast. No gradients doing heavy lifting, no glow, no drop shadows.
2. **Headline.** Uppercase grotesque set in a LIGHT weight (not bold), stacked on 3–4 short lines with tight leading (line-height under 1.0). Cap height is at least 6x the body text size.
3. **Tone per line.** Each headline line is one flat tone. Exactly one line is in the accent colour, one is near-white, the rest are a dim grey that sits close to the ground.
4. **One accent.** A single warm red-orange. On any one screen it appears in at most four places: one headline line, one primary button, and small status pills. Nothing else is coloured.
5. **Product cards.** Products sit as cut-outs on small solid cards (light grey or dark grey), corner radius 18–24px, each with 1–2 tiny pill tags at the top, the product name in regular weight, and one small grey metadata line. Cards dock into the empty corners of the headline and break the column grid; they do not sit in a tidy equal row.
6. **Everything round.** All controls are full pills or circles: segmented toggle, icon buttons, tags. No square-cornered buttons.
7. **Small print.** Supporting labels are tiny (10–12px), uppercase or mono, often prefixed with "//", set in a narrow left column. Telemetry-style mono text appears as an overlay detail.
8. **Glass tiles.** Spec facts sit in one frosted block divided by hairline seams (not separate floating cards): icon on top, label in regular weight, a tiny dim value under it.
9. **Air.** At least a third of the hero frame is empty ground. Text blocks are narrow; nothing is centered by default.
10. **Rows.** Lists are set as hairline-separated rows, not a grid of boxed cards: a large light-weight title sits in a left column, and each row on the right leads with an outsized dim numeral, then a regular-weight title and tiny grey detail lines. A small product card floats in the left column. Row content aligns to shared column edges all the way down.


Note (30 Sep 2026, Zipline blend): the display face is now Anton, heavy and condensed, by the owner's choice to blend with the Zipline system. Where this bar asks for light-weight display type, read it as: one display voice across both sheets, hierarchy carried by colour (dim ink, ember, near-white) rather than by weight.
