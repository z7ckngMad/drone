## How the market sells

Nobody in the sample sells 30-minute or 1-hour slots. They sell battery packages that include a maximum time on site (for example "2 batteries, up to 3 hours"), and charge travel outside the home city separately.

## Recommendation for a new pilot

- Battery packages (current): fine as they are. Mini 4 Pro at Rp450.000 / 750.000 / 950.000 sits about 10–35% under established operators. FPV at Rp500.000 / 850.000 / 1.100.000 / 1.350.000 is in line with the market.
- Time hire (pilot on site, flying on cue): 1 hour Rp900.000 with up to 2 Mini batteries or 4 FPV batteries included; 2 hours Rp1.500.000; each extra hour Rp600.000. This keeps a time slot slightly above the matching battery package, so clients are not tempted to use it as a cheaper way to fly.
- 30 minutes at Rp500.000 (1 Mini battery or 2 FPV batteries included): keep only for jobs close to home. Once travel is counted it is rarely worth it.
- "Full day 4–5 hours from Rp4.500.000" looks high for a new pilot: comparable listings are Rp1.700.000–2.000.000 and the aggregator's 3–5 hour band tops out at Rp4.000.000. Rp2.500.000–3.000.000 is easier to win.
- Add an on-site cap to each battery package (for example 1 hour per 2 batteries) and a travel line for jobs outside the home city, as competitors do.
